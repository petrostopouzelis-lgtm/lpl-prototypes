What the data file holds
  • subject: the person's name, and optionally their role and time in role.
  • report: date, form (TEIQue-360), norm and participant ID.
  • self: their own 20 scores (Global, 4 factors, 15 facets), as percentiles 1 to 99.
  • others: how many raters responded and were invited, and the raters' average for the same 20 scores.
  • groups: each rater group with its counts and scores, and optionally the raters' names.
  • Optional: self_raw (raw scores for the coach table) and settings.
